# Option chain analysis corrections

The existing page and trading safeguards are retained. Analytical panels use the full selected-expiry chain; the nearby-strike filter affects display only.

1. **Buildup classification:** both premium and OI must move beyond configurable `priceNoise` and `oiNoise` thresholds. Either flat input gives `No Change`; absent inputs give `Unavailable`. Daily analysis pairs NSE session premium change with session OI change. Intraday analysis pairs changes in LTP and OI between matched snapshots. Default noise thresholds are zero; nonzero thresholds require instrument-specific calibration.
2. **Classified activity:** CE/PE long buildup, short buildup, covering, and long unwinding have separate contract totals. Each contribution is `abs(deltaOI) * (1 + min(1, abs(deltaOI)/max(1, priorOI))) / (1 + distanceInStrikes) / max(1, totalLocalPriorOI)`. This preserves contract size while normalizing prior OI and discounting distant strikes. Dominance requires a 1.35 ratio. These are inferred categories, without trader identity, cost basis, or trap claims.
3. **PCR:** OI PCR, cumulative session volume PCR, and positive-additions PCR are distinct. Missing inputs or a zero Call denominator yield `null`, displayed as unavailable. Positive-additions PCR divides positive PE additions by positive CE additions; signed net CE/PE changes remain separate. OI PCR contributes directional confluence only when classified activity and underlying direction agree.
4. **Volume:** index records include actual `cVol`/`pVol`. Live participation uses nonnegative differences in cumulative volume between same-session snapshots. Missing or decreasing counters invalidate that interval; the next valid snapshot establishes a new baseline. Neither volume skew nor volume itself identifies trade aggressor direction.
5. **Levels:** strongest OI walls remain concentration references. Nearest nonzero-OI barriers from all candidates constrain risk/reward. Local median strike spacing replaces the mean span. Each wall's state uses aligned OI and premium changes.
6. **Breakouts:** a completed underlying close must cross the previous barrier from the other side, with consecutive completed candles and inferred short covering at that strike. Current spot must remain beyond the barrier. Approaches are watch zones; wall migration alone remains descriptive.
7. **Snapshots:** reactive history uses exchange timestamps, rejects out-of-order timestamps, and resets across contract/session changes. Revisions compare OI, premium, volume, and quotes. Alerts state actual elapsed seconds. Momentum selects an elapsed window of at least 90 seconds, which can be met with four 30-second polls. Synthetic chart anchors and legacy fetch-time history are excluded from analytics. Live analytical signals require fresh sources.
8. **Next-day confirmation:** independent domains are underlying price (up to 40 points), classified positioning including PCR (up to 40), and executable liquidity (20). A setup requires all three domains and a net score of at least ±60, followed by the existing quote, expiry, candle, and risk/reward checks. Cross-sectional CE/PE IV adds no directional points. Max Pain is a payout-minimizing reference with observed distance/migration, without a gravity or automatic mean-reversion vote.

Core calculations: `src/components/OptionChainNew/utils/analysis.js`. Regression coverage: `test/engine/option-chain-analysis.test.js` and the existing option signal/safety tests.

OI describes outstanding contracts with both long and short counterparties, so identity and directional intent cannot be recovered from OI alone. Reference: [OCC Options Industry Council](https://www.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/faq/general-information). NSE displays volume and OI in contracts: [NSE option chain](https://www.nseindia.com/option-chain).
